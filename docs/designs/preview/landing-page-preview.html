How it works

Share it. Forget it. Get it back.

1

Share it

Tap Share in any app and pick LinkHive. It's saved. No form to fill in.

2

Forget it

It waits in your Inbox. Add a category later, or never.

3

Get it back

Every morning LinkHive picks one link. Open it, snooze it or archive it.

Features

Built for the 500th link, not the 5th.

One-tap capture

Saving takes the same tap as any share. Title and preview fill in afterwards.

One link a day

A 9am reminder, a Today screen and a home screen widget bring back one link at a time.

Saved twice? Noted.

Share the same link again and LinkHive merges it and marks it Saved 2×. Those come back first.

Find anything

Search, quick filters and Source / Category / Sort, made for hundreds of links.

Offline first

Works without an account or a connection. Sign in and it syncs, with a screen to settle conflicts.

Speaks your language

English, Hindi, Gujarati and Arabic, right-to-left included. Light and dark.

Maker's first comment posted right at launch

Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm Kunj, an application developer from Rajkot, India. I built LinkHive because I had three piles of saved links (Instagram saves, LinkedIn saves and a chat with myself) and never opened any of them.

Saving was never the problem. Getting links back was. So LinkHive does two things:

  • Share → saved. One tap from any app's share sheet. No form.
  • One link a day. Every morning it picks one link and puts it on a Today screen, a notification and a home screen widget.

If you save the same link twice, it merges them and marks it "Saved 2×". It works fully offline and without an account, and syncs when you sign in. It's in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Arabic.

It's built in Flutter and the code is on GitHub. I'd love to hear what you save and never come back to, and what would make you open it again.
