# LinkHive v1.0.1

The first public release of LinkHive, the link saver that brings your links back.

Share a link from any app and it's saved in one tap. Every morning LinkHive picks one link and puts it on your Today screen, in a notification and on a home screen widget, so your saves stop piling up unread.

## Download

**`LinkHive-v1.0.1.apk`** (60 MB) works on any Android phone running Android 7.0 or newer. iOS: build from source (see the README).

**Installing:** open the APK on your phone and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. Future versions will install as updates over this one.

## What's in it

- **One-tap capture.** Share from Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn or any app; the link lands in your Inbox with no form to fill in.
- **One link a day.** A 9am reminder, a Today screen (Open, Snooze, Archive) and an Android home screen widget.
- **Saved twice? Noted.** Sharing a link you already have merges it and marks it Saved 2×.
- **Find anything.** Search, quick filters (Unread, High, Saved 2×+, Read) and Source / Category / Sort.
- **Offline first.** Works without an account or a connection. Sign in with Google or email to sync, with a screen to settle conflicts.
- **Safe sign-out.** Before you sign out, LinkHive shows what hasn't synced yet. Signing out then clears this phone's copy, so the next person to sign in never sees your links.
- **4 languages.** English, Hindi, Gujarati and Arabic (right-to-left). Light and dark themes.
