How it works

Share it. Forget it. Get it back.

  1. 1

    Share it

    Tap Share in any app and pick LinkHive. It's saved. No form to fill in.

  2. 2

    Forget it

    It waits in your Inbox. Add a category later, or never.

  3. 3

    Get it back

    Every morning LinkHive picks one link. Open it, snooze it or archive it.

Features

Built for the 500th link, not the 5th.

One-tap capture

Saving takes the same tap as any share. Title and preview fill in afterwards.

One link a day

A 9am reminder, a Today screen and a home screen widget bring back one link at a time.

Saved twice? Noted.

Share the same link again and LinkHive merges it and marks it Saved 2×. Those come back first.

Find anything

Search, quick filters and Source / Category / Sort, made for hundreds of links.

Offline first

Works without an account or a connection. Sign in and it syncs, with a screen to settle conflicts.

Speaks your language

English, Hindi, Gujarati and Arabic, right-to-left included. Light and dark.

Screens

Bold, simple, one-handed.

Under the hood

Offline-first Flutter, built to survive bad networks.

Every write lands on the phone first. The cloud catches up when it can, and nothing gets silently overwritten.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • BLoC
  • Hive
  • Firebase Auth
  • Cloud Firestore
  • go_router
  • get_it
  • Jetpack Glance
  • flutter_localizations

Offline-first sync

Hive first, then an outbox queue to Firestore. Works as a guest; signing in uploads what's already there.

Conflict resolution

Field-level conflict records with keep-local, keep-cloud and custom merge.

One place for every action

Today, cards and the widget all open, archive and snooze through one LinkManager.

Native where it counts

Android and iOS share targets, a Glance home screen widget, Custom Tabs for links, 4 locales with RTL.
